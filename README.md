# Kyosaku

A macOS menu bar app that gently nudges you back when what's on your screen drifts away from your current
task. It uses Apple's on-device models, never blocks anything, and keeps everything on your Mac.

> [!NOTE]
> Kyosaku is in early development and is not usable yet.

## Requirements

- macOS 26 or later
- A Mac with Apple silicon and Apple Intelligence turned on
